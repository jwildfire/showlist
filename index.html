      Two ways to use this

      1. Preloaded listings — nothing to set up. Real shows for one metro area, swept from public venue calendars every week and shipped with the site. Just hit Find shows.
      2. Anywhere else — add your own Ticketmaster key in Setup (free, about two minutes) and scan live listings around any city or ZIP.

      Either way, connect Spotify in Setup to turn a lineup into a real playlist in your account. Everything runs in your browser: keys and tokens are stored in this browser's local storage and are never sent anywhere but the service they belong to.

      Setup

      This site has no server, so it uses your keys, kept in your browser. Every field is optional — fill in the ones for the services you want.
